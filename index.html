About

Model: ResNet-18 (ImageNet-pretrained), fc replaced for 10 EuroSAT classes. Input: 64×64 RGB patch normalized with ImageNet mean/std. Runtime: ONNX via onnxruntime-web (local, no network).

Scope note: Prediction is for EuroSAT-style 64×64 Sentinel-2 RGB patches. Accuracy on other imagery is unknown.

The viewer shows 12 example patches (two per class across 6 classes) drawn from the committed EuroSAT test split (splits/test.csv in the source repository). These are a demonstration subset only -- not a measure of accuracy.
